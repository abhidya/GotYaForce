## Lifecycle safety and tests

The genuine frame claim propagates only its private capability through
full→projectile→allocation. Standalone calls on frame-owned actors are rejected.
Runtime brands and graph identity checks reject copied methods, alternate
actual actors and competing source storage. Partial allocation/spawn failures
preserve prior effects and close both the actor lifecycle and allocator. A later
action failure after successful spawn also closes allocation, so the source
ammo debit and child cannot be replayed with changed selectors.
