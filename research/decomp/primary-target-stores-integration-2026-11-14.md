## Arithmetic and failure bounds

The shared live SquareDistance helper remains unchanged. Its exact finite
binary32 model may produce zero from equal coordinates or squared underflow.
It cannot produce a negative squared distance; negative coordinates are valid
inputs but are not evidence of that unreachable result branch.

The horizontal expression deliberately uses separate f32 subtraction, multiply
and addition operations rather than a fused expression or a copied 3D result.
Live coordinates can change after758 is written, allowing a genuine positive
horizontal result even when the prior3D result was zero. Nonfinite operands or
intermediates refuse in source operation order. No sqrt, estimator table,
third-party estimator implementation or new processor assumption is introduced.

Caught reentry or independently failed canonical owners close the outer actor
and scheduler graph permanently. This remains bounded synthetic offline
integration, not original execution, MATCH, retail hydration, a completed frame,
scheduler authority, hardware parity or browser playability.
